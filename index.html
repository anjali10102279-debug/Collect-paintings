How it works

Seven paintings. One quiet question each.

Look slowly. Name the hand that made it. Every painting you recognise is hung on the walls of your own gallery.

  1. 01

    Look

    A painting fills most of the screen. Take your time with it; nothing here is timed.

  2. 02

    Name the artist

    Choose from four names. A wrong guess is forgiven once — you always get a second chance.

  3. 03

    Collect

    Each right answer flies up to My art gallery in the menu. Miss twice and the painting moves on without you.

  4. 04

    Walk your gallery

    After the seventh painting, step inside. Everything you have collected hangs along the left and right walls.
